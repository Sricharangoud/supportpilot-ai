# Northstar Shop Refund Processing Policy

## Refund Timeline
Once your returned package arrives at our fulfillment center, inspection takes 1 to 2 business days. After approval, your refund is issued immediately:
- **Credit / Debit Cards:** 5 to 7 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.
- **PayPal / Apple Pay / Google Pay:** 2 to 3 business days.
- **Northstar Store Credit:** Issued within 2 hours of return inspection approval.

## Partial Refunds
A partial refund (up to 20% restocking fee) may apply if an item is returned without original packaging, shows signs of wear, or is missing non-essential accessories.

## Original Shipping Costs
Original expedited or overnight shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a Northstar Shop fulfillment error or a defective product.

## Missing Refunds
If more than 7 business days have passed since you received your refund confirmation email, please check with your bank or card issuer first. If the funds still do not appear, contact support with your order number and refund reference ID.
