# Northstar Shop Warranty Coverage

## 1-Year Limited Warranty
All Northstar-branded hardware and electronics include a **1-year limited warranty** from the original date of delivery. This covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal consumer use.

## What Is Not Covered
The warranty does not cover:
- Accidental damage (drops, liquid spills, cracked screens)
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, battery degradation over time)
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Damage caused by using non-approved third-party power adapters

## Filing a Warranty Claim
To file a warranty claim, provide your order number, serial number, and a brief description or video of the defect. Approved claims are resolved via free repair, replacement, or Northstar store credit if the item is out of stock.
