# Northstar Shop Return Policy

## 30-Day Return Window
Northstar Shop offers a 30-day return window from the date of delivery for most new, unopened, and unworn items. Items must be returned in their original packaging with all tags, manuals, and accessories attached.

## Return Shipping Fees
- Standard returns within the United States incur a flat $6.95 return shipping label fee, which is deducted from your refund.
- Returns for store credit are free of charge (the $6.95 fee is waived).
- Defective, damaged, or incorrect items shipped by Northstar Shop always qualify for free return shipping.

## Non-Returnable Items
The following items are final sale and cannot be returned:
- Gift cards and downloadable digital products
- Personalized or custom-engraved merchandise
- Opened personal care, grooming, or hygiene products
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale" on the product page

## How to Start a Return
Log in to your Northstar Shop account, go to **Orders**, select the order, and click **Request Return**. You will receive a prepaid printable QR code or shipping label via email within 15 minutes.
