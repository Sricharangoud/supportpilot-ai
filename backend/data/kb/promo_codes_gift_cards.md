# Promo Codes & Gift Cards

## Promo Code Rules
- Only **one** promo code may be applied per order; promo codes cannot be stacked.
- Promo codes do not apply to gift cards, shipping fees, taxes, or "Final Sale" clearance items.
- Forgot to apply a code? Contact support within 24 hours of placing your order and we can retroactively apply a valid, active promo code once per customer.

## Northstar Gift Cards
- Digital and physical gift cards never expire and carry zero maintenance fees.
- You can check your gift card balance under **My Account > Gift Cards** or by entering the 16-digit card number at checkout.
- Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be redeemed for cash unless required by state law.
