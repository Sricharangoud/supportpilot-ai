# Customer Support Hours & Escalation SLA

## Live Support Hours
- **Monday – Friday:** 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM EST
- **Saturday – Sunday:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST
- Closed on major US federal holidays (New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day).

## Human Escalation Response Times
When an inquiry is escalated to a human specialist:
- **Standard Support Tickets:** Response within 24 business hours.
- **Urgent / Damaged Order Tickets:** Response within 4 business hours during operating hours.
- **Peak Tier Loyalty Members:** Priority queue with 2-hour response SLA.
