# Account Management & Security

## Creating & Updating Your Account
You can create a Northstar Shop account with your email address or sign in using Google or Apple SSO. You can update your saved addresses, payment methods, and communication preferences in **Account Settings**.

## Password Reset
Click **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page to receive a password reset link. Reset links expire after 30 minutes. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within 5 minutes.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
We strongly recommend enabling 2FA using an authenticator app or SMS in **Account Settings > Security**.

## Deleting Your Account
To permanently delete your account, go to **Account Settings > Privacy > Delete Account**. Note that deleting your account forfeits any unused Northstar Rewards points.
