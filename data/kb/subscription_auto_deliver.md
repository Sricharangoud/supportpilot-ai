# Auto-Deliver Subscription Policy

## Subscription Benefits
Subscribe to Auto-Deliver on eligible consumables and accessories to save **10% on every recurring order** and receive free standard shipping regardless of order total.

## Managing Your Subscription
In **My Account > Subscriptions**, you can:
- Change delivery frequency (every 30, 60, or 90 days)
- Skip an upcoming shipment
- Pause or cancel your subscription anytime

## Cutoff Window
Changes or cancellations must be made at least **48 hours before** your scheduled renewal date. Once a renewal order has been billed and processed, standard return policies apply.
