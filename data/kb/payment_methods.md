# Accepted Payment Methods & Billing Issues

## Payment Methods Accepted
Northstar Shop accepts:
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit/debit cards
- PayPal and PayPal Pay in 4
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Klarna and Affirm (available on orders over $50 for eligible US residents)
- Northstar Shop Gift Cards

## Sales Tax
Applicable state and local sales tax is calculated automatically at checkout based on your shipping destination.

## Declined or Failed Payments
If your payment is declined:
1. Verify your billing zip code and CVV match your card issuer's records.
2. Check if your bank blocked the transaction for fraud verification.
3. Temporary pending authorizations from failed attempts drop off automatically within 3 to 5 business days.
