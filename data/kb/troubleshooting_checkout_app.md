# Troubleshooting Checkout & Mobile App Issues

## Checkout Page Errors
If you experience errors during checkout:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies, or try an Incognito/Private window.
2. Disable ad blockers or VPNs temporarily, as they can interfere with 3D Secure payment verification.
3. Ensure your browser is updated to the latest version.

## 3D Secure / OTP Verification Failures
If your bank's One-Time Passcode (OTP) window fails to load, try switching from mobile data to Wi-Fi (or vice versa) or use PayPal/Apple Pay as an alternative payment method.

## Mobile App Crashes
Update the Northstar Shop app to the latest version in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. If crashes persist on iOS or Android, reinstall the app and sign back in.
