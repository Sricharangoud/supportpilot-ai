# Northstar Shop International Shipping Policy

## Supported Countries
Northstar Shop ships to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, and Singapore.

## Delivery Estimates & Rates
- **International Standard (7–14 business days):** Calculated at checkout based on weight and destination, starting at $19.95.
- **International Express (3–6 business days):** Starting at $39.95 via DHL Express.

## Customs, Duties, and Taxes
International orders may be subject to import duties, VAT, or customs fees levied by the destination country. Unless "Duties & Taxes Prepaid (DDP)" is selected at checkout, these fees are the responsibility of the recipient upon delivery.

## Restricted Items
Lithium-ion battery packs over 100Wh and pressurized aerosols cannot be shipped internationally due to air freight regulations.
