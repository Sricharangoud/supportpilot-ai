# Privacy & Data Security Policy

## Payment & Data Security
Northstar Shop uses TLS 1.3 encryption for all web traffic and is **PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant**. We never store full credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers.

## GDPR & CCPA Rights
Customers in the EU, UK, and California (and all US states) may request:
- A copy of their personal data (exported within 15 days)
- Correction of inaccurate personal information
- Complete deletion of personal data (subject to legal tax/fraud retention requirements)

## Submitting a Privacy Request
You can submit a data export or deletion request in **Account Settings > Privacy** or by emailing our Data Protection Officer.
