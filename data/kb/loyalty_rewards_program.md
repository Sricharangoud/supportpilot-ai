# Northstar Rewards Loyalty Program

## How It Works
Joining Northstar Rewards is free. Members earn **1 point per $1 spent** on eligible purchases (excluding tax and shipping).

## Membership Tiers
- **Explorer (0–499 pts/year):** 1x points, birthday gift, early access to seasonal sales.
- **Summit (500–1,499 pts/year):** 1.25x points, free standard shipping on orders over $35.
- **Peak (1,500+ pts/year):** 1.5x points, free expedited shipping on all orders, dedicated priority support.

## Redeeming Points
Points can be redeemed at checkout in increments of 100 points ($5 reward). Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.
