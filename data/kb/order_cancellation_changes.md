# Order Cancellation & Modification Policy

## 1-Hour Cancellation Window
You may cancel your order for a full refund within 1 hour of placing it by going to **My Account > Orders > Cancel Order**. After 1 hour, orders enter automated warehouse fulfillment and can no longer be canceled.

## Changing Shipping Address
Shipping addresses can only be updated within 1 hour of purchase and before a shipping label is generated. Once the carrier picks up the package, we cannot reroute it; you may use UPS My Choice or FedEx Delivery Manager to request a hold at a local pickup point.

## Modifying Items or Quantities
We cannot add, remove, or swap items in an existing order. If you are within the 1-hour window, cancel the order and place a new one. Otherwise, wait for delivery and initiate a standard return.
