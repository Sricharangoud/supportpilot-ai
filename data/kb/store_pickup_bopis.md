# Store Pickup (BOPIS) Policy

## How Buy Online, Pick Up In Store Works
Select **Free Store Pickup** at checkout for eligible in-stock items at participating Northstar Shop retail locations.

## Ready Time & Pickup Window
- Orders placed at least 3 hours before store closing are typically ready within **4 hours**. Wait for your "Ready for Pickup" email before heading to the store.
- Orders are held for **7 calendar days**. Unclaimed orders are automatically canceled and refunded to your original payment method.

## What to Bring
Bring a valid government-issued photo ID and your order confirmation email. If someone else is picking up your order, add them as an alternate pickup person during checkout.
