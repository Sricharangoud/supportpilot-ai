# Northstar Shop Domestic Shipping Policy

## Order Processing Time
Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday through Friday ship the same business day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST, on weekends, or on federal holidays ship the next business day.

## Domestic Shipping Tiers (Contiguous US)
- **Standard Ground Shipping (3–5 business days):** Free on orders of $75 or more; $7.95 for orders under $75.
- **Expedited 2-Day Shipping (2 business days):** $14.95 flat rate.
- **Overnight Express (1 business day):** $29.95 flat rate (available for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST).

## Alaska, Hawaii, and US Territories
Shipments to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and APO/FPO addresses ship via USPS Priority Mail and typically arrive in 5 to 9 business days. Overnight shipping is not available for these destinations.

## PO Boxes
Standard Ground orders under 15 lbs can be delivered to PO Boxes via USPS. Expedited and Overnight shipments require a physical street address.
