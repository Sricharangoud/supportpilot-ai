# Order Tracking & Lost Packages

## How to Track Your Order
Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email containing your tracking link. You can also view live tracking status anytime in **My Account > Orders**.

## Tracking Number Not Updating
Carriers (UPS, FedEx, USPS) can take up to 24 hours after label creation to scan the package and display movement. If there is no update after 48 business hours, reach out to our support team.

## Package Marked Delivered But Not Received
If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you cannot locate the package:
1. Wait 24 hours, as carriers occasionally scan packages early.
2. Check around porches, garages, mailrooms, and with neighbors.
3. If still missing after 24 hours, contact Northstar Shop within 7 days of the delivery scan so we can open a carrier trace and arrange a replacement or refund.
