# Price Adjustment & Price Match Policy

## 14-Day Price Adjustment
If an item you purchased on Northstar Shop drops in price on our website within **14 days** of your purchase date, we will refund the difference to your original payment method or as store credit.

## Exclusions
Price adjustments do not apply to:
- Flash sales, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, or holiday doorbusters
- Clearance or "Final Sale" items
- Third-party marketplace sellers or unauthorized resellers
- Promo code discounts that were released after your purchase

## How to Request
Contact support with your order number and a link to the current product page showing the lower price.
