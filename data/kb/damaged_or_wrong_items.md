# Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

## 48-Hour Reporting Window
If your order arrives damaged, defective, or with an incorrect/missing item, please report it within **48 hours of delivery** (and no later than 7 days) so we can resolve it immediately.

## Required Information
Please have your order number and clear photos showing:
1. The damaged or incorrect item
2. The shipping box and packing slip

## Resolution
Northstar Shop will send a free replacement via expedited shipping at no cost to you, or issue a full refund to your original payment method. You do not need to pay return shipping for damaged or wrong items.
